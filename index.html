Why Bowldyna

Not just where the ball went—why it went there.

Bowldyna Studio combines release conditions, ball properties, lane oil, oil transition, and pin interaction in one simulator. It is built for bowlers who want to understand misses, compare adjustments, and find a more repeatable route to the pocket.

Core capabilities

From ball motion to pinfall

01

Strike-route search

Search combinations of speed, starting board, launch angle, rev rate, axis rotation, and axis tilt. Candidates are ranked with nearby-shot robustness testing rather than a single ideal line.

02

Continuous ball-motion model

Visualize skid, hook, and roll as a continuous motion shaped by speed, rotation, coverstock, surface, RG, differential, PAP, layout, and oil conditions.

03

2D and Advanced 3D Beta pin physics

Use fast 2D physics for everyday analysis or switch to the Advanced 3D Beta rigid-body model for detailed pin interaction. Displayed pinfall and scoring use the same mode-specific judgement.

04

Oil transition editing

Model breakdown and carrydown with editable zones, width, intensity, distance, and fade. Compare how the same release changes as the lane develops.

05

Ball and lane libraries

Save your own bowling balls and lane conditions, then load them directly into the simulator for faster comparison and repeatable testing.

06

Metrics and diagnostics

Review breakpoint, pocket board, entry angle, oil-contact transitions, hit pins, fallen pins, remaining pins, and diagnostic information for each result.

投球を、感覚からロジックへ。

Bowldyna Studioは、速度・回転・投球ライン・ボール特性・レーンオイル・ピン物理を組み合わせて、軌道とピンアクションを可視化する物理ベースのボウリングシミュレーターです。

ストライク軌道を逆算

現在のボールとレーン条件から、速度・板目・角度・回転の候補を探索し、周辺誤差への強さも含めて評価します。

2D／3D Betaピン物理

高速な2Dと、詳細な3D剛体モデルを切り替えられます。各モードで、画面表示と残りピン判定が一致するよう設計されています。

オイル変化まで検証

ブレイクダウンとキャリーダウンを編集し、レーン変化によって同じ投球がどう変わるかを比較できます。
