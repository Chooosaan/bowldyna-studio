Turn feel into logic.

Bowldyna Studio is a physics-based bowling simulator that visualizes ball motion, entry angle, and pin action. Adjust release and lane conditions to explore why each shot behaves differently.

The core trajectory simulation is available for free. A one-time Bowldyna Pro purchase permanently unlocks advanced release settings, ball properties, oil controls, pin physics, pin setup editing, and saved profiles. No ads. No subscription.
